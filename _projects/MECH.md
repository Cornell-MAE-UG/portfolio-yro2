As part of the MAE 3780 course (Mechatronics), this project focuses on designing, assembling, and programming an autonomous cube-collecting robot built upon a Boe-Bot chassis. The system relies on QTI sensors for boundary detection and L9110H H-bridge motor drivers to control continuous rotation servo motors. These types of embedded systems projects are critical in the broader field of automated mechatronics and robotics, where prioritizing mechanical simplicity and reliable hardware-software interfacing is essential for consistent autonomous operation.
